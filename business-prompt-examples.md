# 💬 Business Prompt Examples for AI Evaluation
### Real-World Small Business Scenarios — Kamau General Supplies

---

## About These Prompts
These prompts are designed based on real financial data from **Kamau General Supplies**, a retail shop in Kenya. They represent genuine challenges faced by small business owners and are used to test how well AI chatbots understand and respond to business problems.

---

## Category 1: Cash Flow Problems

### Prompt 1.1 — Declining Balance
> *"My shop makes sales every month. In January I made KES 26,800 in sales, February KES 25,000, March KES 28,800. But every month my bank balance goes down. My opening balance was KES 50,000 and now it is KES 26,400. Why is this happening and what should I do?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Explain the concept of net cash flow (sales ≠ profit)
- Identify that expenses exceed income each month
- Suggest reviewing the biggest expense (inventory/stock)
- Recommend a simple budget adjustment

---

### Prompt 1.2 — High Inventory Costs
> *"I spend about KES 21,500 every month just buying stock for my shop. My total income is around KES 26,800. After paying rent (KES 8,000), electricity (KES 1,500), and transport (KES 800), I am losing money. What is wrong with my business model?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Calculate the cost-to-revenue ratio
- Explain that rent + stock alone exceeds revenue
- Suggest pricing strategy review
- Recommend cost negotiation with suppliers

---

## Category 2: Daily Operations

### Prompt 2.1 — Sales Tracking
> *"I sell maize flour, cooking oil, sugar, and rice in my shop. I want to know which product makes me the most money. How should I track this and what tool can I use?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Suggest a simple spreadsheet tracking method
- Recommend recording quantity sold × price per unit
- Mention calculating profit margin per product
- Suggest free tools (Google Sheets, Excel)

---

### Prompt 2.2 — Expense Management
> *"Every month I have miscellaneous expenses that I cannot explain. In May it was KES 500, April KES 350, March KES 400. How do I control these small expenses that add up?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Advise keeping receipts for every expense
- Suggest creating expense categories
- Recommend a daily expense log
- Provide a simple template example

---

## Category 3: Business Growth

### Prompt 3.1 — Expansion Planning
> *"I have been running my general supplies shop for 5 months. My best month was April with KES 31,400 in sales. I want to open a second shop. Is this a good idea based on my finances?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Point out the negative cash flow trend first
- Advise stabilizing current operations before expanding
- Suggest building an emergency fund
- Provide criteria for when expansion makes sense

---

### Prompt 3.2 — Market Research
> *"I want to add new products to my shop. I currently sell maize flour, cooking oil, sugar, and rice. What other products should I add that would sell well in a general supplies shop in Kenya?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Suggest complementary products (tea, salt, toiletries)
- Recommend surveying existing customers
- Advise considering profit margin vs. shelf life
- Mention seasonal demand patterns

---

## Category 4: Financial Planning

### Prompt 4.1 — Budgeting
> *"I want to create a budget for my shop for the next 3 months. My average income is KES 27,000 per month and my average expenses are KES 32,000. How do I create a realistic budget?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Acknowledge the budget gap immediately
- Walk through a simple budget template
- Prioritize expenses (needs vs. wants)
- Suggest income-increase strategies alongside cost-cutting

---

### Prompt 4.2 — Savings Strategy
> *"My shop closing balance has been going down every month. From KES 50,000 in January to KES 26,400 in June. At this rate, when will I run out of money and what should I do now?"*

**What a good AI response should cover:**
- Calculate the approximate burn rate (~KES 4,700/month)
- Estimate months remaining (~5–6 months)
- Stress urgency of action
- Provide 3–5 concrete immediate steps

---

## Evaluation Notes

When using these prompts to evaluate AI chatbots:
1. Use **the same prompt** across all chatbots (no modifications)
2. Allow **maximum 5 conversation turns** per session
3. Rate each response using the [AI Evaluation Rubric](./ai-evaluation-rubric.md)
4. Record the **full conversation transcript**
5. Submit your **comparative evaluation** with justification
