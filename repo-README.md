## 🏪 Business Case Study: Kamau General Supplies

A real-world general supplies shop used as the basis for AI evaluation tasks.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Business Type | General Supplies / Retail |
| Currency | KES (Kenyan Shillings) |
| Data Period | January – May 2025 |
| Opening Balance | KES 50,000 |
| Monthly Revenue (avg) | KES ~27,380 |
| Key Expense | Stock/Inventory (~66% of outflows) |

### 📉 Key Business Insight
The business is experiencing **negative net cash flow every month** (Jan: -5,500 | Feb: -4,400 | Mar: -3,850 | Apr: -4,350 | May: -5,500), meaning outflows consistently exceed inflows. This is a critical scenario used to test whether AI chatbots give **accurate, useful, and actionable** financial advice to small business owners.

## 🤖 AI Evaluation Framework

### What I Evaluate
When comparing AI chatbot responses for small business queries, I assess:

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Is the response easy to understand for a non-expert? |
| **Accuracy** | Is the financial/business advice factually correct? |
| **Usefulness** | Does it solve the business owner's actual problem? |
| **Completeness** | Does it address all parts of the question? |
| **Tone** | Is it appropriate for a small business owner? |

### Scoring Scale
- ⭐ 1 – Poor: Incorrect or unhelpful
- ⭐⭐ 2 – Fair: Partially useful
- ⭐⭐⭐ 3 – Good: Mostly accurate and helpful
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 – Very Good: Clear, accurate, actionable
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 – Excellent: Exceptional quality across all dimensions

### Inventory Management
> *"I spend over 65% of my money on restocking. Is this too much? How can I manage my inventory better?"*
