# 🧪 AI Response Evaluation Rubric
### Small Business Use Cases — Structured Evaluation Guide

---

## Purpose
This rubric provides a structured framework for comparing and evaluating responses from multiple AI chatbots on small business queries. It ensures consistent, objective, and useful feedback.

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Clarity (1–5)
Does the AI explain concepts in simple, business-owner-friendly language?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Crystal clear, no jargon, easy for anyone to understand |
| 4 | Mostly clear with minor technical terms explained |
| 3 | Understandable but could be simpler |
| 2 | Somewhat confusing or overly technical |
| 1 | Unclear or incomprehensible |

---

### 2. Accuracy (1–5)
Is the financial or business information factually correct?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | All facts, figures, and advice are correct |
| 4 | Mostly accurate with minor errors |
| 3 | Generally correct but missing key details |
| 2 | Contains some incorrect information |
| 1 | Largely inaccurate or misleading |

---

### 3. Usefulness (1–5)
Does the response solve the actual business problem?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Highly actionable, directly solves the problem |
| 4 | Useful with practical suggestions |
| 3 | Somewhat helpful but generic |
| 2 | Limited practical value |
| 1 | Not helpful at all |

---

### 4. Completeness (1–5)
Does the response address all parts of the question?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Fully addresses all aspects of the query |
| 4 | Addresses most parts |
| 3 | Addresses the main point but misses sub-questions |
| 2 | Partially answers the question |
| 1 | Fails to answer the core question |

---

### 5. Tone (1–5)
Is the response appropriate for a small business owner?

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Professional, empathetic, and encouraging |
| 4 | Appropriate and respectful |
| 3 | Neutral, neither good nor bad |
| 2 | Too formal, dismissive, or condescending |
| 1 | Inappropriate or off-putting |

## Real Example: Cash Flow Query

**Query:** *"My shop has been making sales every month but my closing balance keeps going down. What is the problem?"*

**Expected Good Response Should Include:**
- Identification that outflows exceed inflows (negative net cash flow)
- Explanation of the difference between revenue and profit
- Actionable advice: reduce stock costs, track expenses, negotiate rent
- Simple language appropriate for a shop owner

**Red Flags in AI Responses:**
- Giving generic advice without addressing the specific pattern
- Using financial jargon without explanation
- Suggesting unrealistic solutions (e.g. "get a bank loan" without context)
- Ignoring the emotional concern of the business owner
